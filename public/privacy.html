Privacy Policy & Data Safety

How OpenRemove protects your data, media, and infrastructure privacy.

Zero Third-Party AI APIs & No External Cloud Data Sharing

OpenRemove never connects to or proxies your images through third-party cloud APIs. All AI segmentation models run directly on private infrastructure using ONNX Runtime.

Ephemeral Storage & Automated File Sweeping (TTL)

When using OpenRemove:

  • Your images are transmitted securely directly to the processing engine.
  • Uploaded files and transparent PNG results are stored in an isolated, sandboxed directory solely to allow you to download them.
  • An automated background retention sweeper permanently deletes all temporary files and job metadata after 30 minutes.
  • We do not index, retain, train on, or monetize your images.

Self-Hosted & Air-Gapped Local Deployments

When self-hosting OpenRemove on your own workstation or private intranet server, 100% of execution occurs locally. No internet connection is required after setup, ensuring absolute privacy for sensitive workflows.

No Tracking, No Advertising, & No Cookies

OpenRemove does not set any advertising, tracking, or cross-site profiling cookies. We collect zero user fingerprints, behavioral trackers, or analytics scripts.
