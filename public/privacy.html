Privacy Policy & Data Safety

How OpenRemove protects your data, media, and infrastructure privacy.

100% Self-Contained Local Execution

OpenRemove does not connect to any external cloud APIs, tracking servers, or telemetry endpoints. All neural network weights run locally on your self-hosted host machine using ONNX Runtime.

Zero Data Retention

Images uploaded for processing are processed in memory and written temporarily only for the duration of the request. No images are indexed, trained on, or shared.

No Tracking & No Cookies

OpenRemove does not set any advertising, tracking, or cross-site tracking cookies. We collect zero user fingerprints, IP tracking logs, or analytics.

Enterprise & Offline Air-Gapped Ready

Because all dependencies and ONNX models are stored locally, OpenRemove can be operated inside air-gapped private intranet environments without internet connectivity.
