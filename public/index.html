How to Use

1. Select Image: Click Upload Image, drag and drop a file, or press Ctrl + V to paste from clipboard.

2. Automatic Processing: BiRefNet AI isolates the foreground subject and removes background automatically.

3. Compare & Download: Drag the Before/After slider to inspect the cutout, then click Download PNG.

API Endpoint

Upload endpoint for external bots and backend services:

POST http://localhost:3000/api/upload
Content-Type: multipart/form-data
Field: image (File)

Realtime SSE Stream:

GET http://localhost:3000/api/process-stream/:jobId
